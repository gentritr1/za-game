THE CHAIN
+4 → +6 → +10

Each counter-slam lands 40ms harder: card scales 1.12 → 1, table shakes 3px, the running total stamps bigger and redder over the pile. Grease dots spray from the impact and stay on the formica for the rest of the round.

sfx: wet slap + oven-door clang, pitched up a semitone per link. On resolve: a long defeated sigh.
THE SHOUT
ZA!

Fires from the button, not the centre — it belongs to you. 150ms pop to 1.25, 120ms settle, cheese-string flecks fly off the Z. Beat the timer and a green NICE sticker slaps under it.

sfx: one hoarse human "ZA!" + register ding.
THE CATCH
SAL FORGOT!

Their name buzzes at 900ms — fast enough to feel like a timer running out. Everyone else's CALL OUT button lights amber. Catch them and two cards drop into their hand one at a time, with a pause between, so they feel each one.

sfx: fluorescent buzz loop → a single sharp whistle on the catch.
VOICE — counter-guy deadpan, short, a little rude "Sal, you're up. Whole line's waiting." "Nothing matches? Grab a slice off the dough pile." "Bot Vito took six cards and no longer respects you." "Table's yours. Somebody wipe it down."
BACKGROUND GAGS

A HELP WANTED sign that never comes down. The cheese shaker with one hole taped over. A wall clock stopped at 2:47. Bot avatars are all the same guy in different hats.

EFFECTS — THE CHAIN, THE SHOUT, THE CATCH
THE CHAIN
+4 → +6 → +10

Every link brightens the whole room: the oven glow expands, the ambient magenta rises one step, the ticker turns solid. The total floats above the pile in white, humming. When it finally lands, all the light snaps out for 90ms — then comes back dimmer.

sfx: neon transformer hum rising in pitch, one bass thud per slam, glass-shatter tink on resolve.
THE SHOUT
ZA!

The word switches on like a sign — 60ms of flicker before it holds, the way a real tube does. Rings of light push out past the table edge. Shout inside 800ms of playing and the tube stays lit for the rest of the round behind your seat.

sfx: tube-strike click, then hum, then the shout on top.
THE CATCH
SAL FORGOT!

A dying-tube stutter on their name, 700ms loop. Their seat light drops to almost nothing. The catch is a spotlight: everything else goes black for 200ms and only the guilty seat is lit while two cards slide in.

sfx: failing-ballast buzz → hard relay clack on the catch.
VOICE — late-night, a little theatrical, never mean "Sal. The oven's waiting and so is everybody else." "Nothing fits. Take one off the dough and think about your life." "Bot Vito takes six. Bot Vito does not blink." "Lights out. Gina's got the last slice."
BACKGROUND GAGS

A neon "OPEN" with the E out. Reflection of a taxi crossing the window every ~40s. The cannoli case is always empty. One tube above the anchovy seat never stops flickering.

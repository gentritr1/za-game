Sunday sauce

Sunday the shutter stayed down till four, and the pot went on at seven. Nobody touched it — not the spoon, not the knob. By the time the jukebox took its first quarter, the whole block knew what day it was. — Nonna Pina, behind the counter since before the Formica

Sugo della domenica

SERVES 8 · 3 HOURS · LOWEST FLAME

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 1/4 c. olive oil
  • 1 lb pork neck bones
  • 1 onion, chopped fine
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 2 cans tomatoes, 28 oz
  • 1 bunch basil · salt

WHAT YOU DO

  1. Brown the pork. Take it out.
  2. Onion soft, garlic thirty seconds.
  3. Tomatoes and pork in, half a can of water.
  4. Lowest flame, lid ajar, three hours.
  5. Basil and salt at the end, never the start.

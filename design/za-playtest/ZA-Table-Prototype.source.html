HOW TO PLAY

On your turn, match the topping or the number/action on the pie. A Wild always works.
  1. Find your turn. Your seat says YOUR TURN; one seat says NEXT. CW or CCW shows play direction.
  2. Choose a card. Raised cards with ✓ are playable. Activate a lower card to hear exactly what must match.
  3. Or draw one. If it matches, play it or keep it and pass. If it does not, your turn ends.
  4. Read the effect. Skip misses the next player; +2 makes them draw two and miss; Reverse flips direction; Wild lets you choose the topping.
  5. Call ZA. When you have two cards, call ZA before playing down to one. Forgetting can cost two cards.

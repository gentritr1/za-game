Sfincione, nine squares

My mother’s tray, the one with the burnt corner, came over on the boat. Tuesday the sfincione goes in it and the counter takes the lot before the fan finishes a turn. I cut nine, I sell eight, and the ninth is in wax paper by the register. — Vito, who is saving it

Sfincione

ONE SHEET PAN · NINE SQUARES

WHAT YOU NEED

  • 1 batch Paulie’s dough
  • 1 large onion, sliced thin
  • 6 anchovy fillets
  • 1 can tomatoes, 28 oz
  • 4 oz caciocavallo, diced
  • 1/2 c. breadcrumbs · oregano

WHAT YOU DO

  1. Onion slow in oil. Anchovy in till it melts.
  2. Tomatoes in, 40 minutes, thick.
  3. Dough into an oiled pan. Rest one hour.
  4. Cheese, then sauce, then crumbs.
  5. 425°F, bottom rack, 25 minutes.
